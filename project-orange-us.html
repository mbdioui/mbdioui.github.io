Project Details

Native Android applications for industrial process digitalisation (smart building) at Orange. Maintenance and enhancement with conversational chatbot integration.

Missions & Features

DialogFlow Chatbot:
Integrated a DialogFlow chatbot into an existing application, adding conversational capabilities for field technicians.

Architecture Modernisation:
Introduced Jetpack ViewModel, DataBinding, LiveData and Coroutines for a reactive, maintainable codebase. Applied Clean Architecture principles.

R&D and Analysis:
Collaborated in R&D workshops to analyse feasibility and propose technical solutions aligned with Orange's design charter.
